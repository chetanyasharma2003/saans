## FAQ

**Q: What happens to my v1.0.0 account?**
A: It works perfectly in v2.0.0. All data preserved. Zero migration steps needed.

**Q: Are there breaking changes?**
A: No. 100% backward compatible. All APIs, data structures, and features from v1.0.0 work in v2.0.0.

**Q: Will v2.0.0 be more expensive?**
A: Same pricing as v1.0.0. Same tiers: Free, ₹299/month, ₹499/month.

**Q: Can I still use v1.0.0?**
A: No, it's deprecated. But migration to v2.0.0 is automatic and free.

**Q: When will v3.0.0 come out?**
A: Not planned. v2.0.0 is designed for 5-10 years of development.

**Q: Who are the real doctors?**
A: Licensed therapists/psychiatrists verified in our system. All credentials checked.

**Q: Is my data really secure?**

**Q: Is there a free version?**
A: Yes, free tier with limited features. Upgrade anytime.
